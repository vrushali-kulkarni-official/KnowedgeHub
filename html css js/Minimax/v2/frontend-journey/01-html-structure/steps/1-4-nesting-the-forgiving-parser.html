Act 1 · Step 1.4

Nesting & the Forgiving Parser

Understand how the browser builds the tree, and why your source and the DOM can disagree.

What to notice

  • The browser never tells you it changed your code. The Elements panel is the only place the truth is visible.
  • Auto-inserted <tbody> (around table rows) and auto-closed <p> are the two you'll hit most.

Live demo — the parser's opinion

The markup below is deliberately broken. Look at the source, then look at the Elements panel and compare:

<p>Outer paragraph <p>Inner paragraph</p></p>

Compare what you see rendered here with the DOM. They are not the same structure — and the source you just read above is exactly what I wrote.

Challenge — Break it on purpose

Write each of these, then inspect the DOM and write down what the browser did and why:

  1. <p>One<p>Two</p></p>
  2. <ul><li>A<li>B</ul>
  3. <div><span>text</div>
  4. <strong>bold <p>not bold?</p></strong>
  5. A <table> with <tr> and <td> but no <tbody>

For each: did the DOM match your source? If not, what did the browser add, remove, or move?

Stuck? Open the hints
  • Case 4: <strong> is inline, so a block <p> inside it gets ejected.
  • Case 5: the browser inserts <tbody> for you. It's implied by the spec, and every modern browser does it.
