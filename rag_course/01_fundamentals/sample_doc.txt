ORBITAL AI — INTERNAL COMPANY HANDBOOK (v3.2)
Last updated: March 2025

Section 1: Company Overview
Orbital AI is a deep-tech research company founded in 2021 and headquartered
in Bengaluru, India. We specialize in building autonomous systems for
satellite constellation management. Our flagship product, StarTrack-9, is
currently used by three national space agencies for real-time orbital debris
avoidance.

Section 2: Leadership
- CEO: Priya Rao
- CTO: Marcus Holloway
- Head of Research: Dr. Aisha Patel
- VP of Engineering: Tomohiro Sato

Section 3: Products
StarTrack-9 is a closed-loop control system that uses reinforcement learning
to coordinate satellite maneuvers. It processes roughly 4.2 million telemetry
points per second. The current version (v4.1) was released in Q1 2025 and
reduced false-positive maneuver triggers by 78% compared to v3.

Section 4: Funding
The company has raised $42M across two rounds. Series A was led by Helios
Ventures in 2023. Series B closed in late 2024 with participation from
Sequoia India and a strategic investment from the Indian Space Research
Organization (ISRO).

Section 5: Office Locations
- HQ: Bengaluru, India (4 floors, 220 employees)
- Research Lab: Toulouse, France (12 researchers)
- Field Office: Houston, Texas (5 employees, NASA liaison)

Section 6: Core Values
1. Precision over speed.
2. Open peer review of all algorithms.
3. No dark satellites — every system we build must be ground-observable.

Section 7: Recent News (March 2025)
Orbital AI signed a multi-year contract with the European Space Agency (ESA)
to deploy StarTrack-9 across the Galileo constellation. The deployment is
scheduled for early 2026.
